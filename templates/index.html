Welcome to Grandpa's Drum

A small beginning.
A lasting impact.

Some instruments are too full of possibility to sit forgotten.

Grandpa's Drum is a philanthropic initiative of Fast Hands Drum Studio. We collect used percussion instruments, restore them to playable condition, and work to place them with individuals, schools, and community organizations who need them.

Discover why we started ↗
EST. IN MEMORY
OF A TRADITION

A tradition worth continuing

It started with my grandfather.

He saw more than worn heads and aging hardware. He saw instruments that could be repaired, music that could still be made, and students who deserved a chance to learn.

He restored old drums and sold them to students at deeply discounted prices—not to make a profit, but to make sure they had working equipment.

Grandpa's Drum carries that simple generosity forward.
