Give a Drum New PurposeOur Story
Why we exist

It started with a grandfather and a drum.

My grandfather understood that a drum was more than an object. It was an invitation: to practice, to listen, to belong, and to discover what you could become.

He repaired instruments and sold them to students at deeply reduced prices, making sure that anyone eager to learn had a working drum in their hands.

Grandpa's Drum carries that idea forward. We believe that a forgotten instrument can become the beginning of somebody else's story.

How it works

Three steps. One enduring purpose.

01 — COLLECT

Give

We identify used drums and percussion gear with more music left in them.

02 — RESTORE

Repair

We assess, clean, and repair instruments so they are ready to be played again.

03 — REDISTRIBUTE

Play

We aim to connect restored instruments with schools, organizations, and individuals who can put them to use.

A few good questions

Before the music begins.

What kinds of instruments will you accept?

We are developing intake guidelines for drums, percussion equipment, and related hardware. Acceptance will depend on condition, repairability, and current needs.

Where will Grandpa's Drum operate?

We're starting in New York's Hudson Valley, with an ambition to build connections and local hubs farther afield.

Is Grandpa's Drum a registered nonprofit?

Not currently. Grandpa's Drum is an initiative of Fast Hands Drum Studio and is not presently a registered nonprofit. Please do not assume donations are tax-deductible.

How can I get involved?

We are preparing ways to contribute instruments, help with repairs, and connect us with music programs. More details will be posted here as these processes open.
