About this website.

Grandpa's Drum is an initiative operated by Fast Hands Drum Studio, initially serving the Hudson Valley region of New York. This notice describes the public website, its instrument donation inquiry form, its first-stage instrument request applicant information form, and its general contact form.

Instrument donation inquiries

Our Donate an Instrument page embeds a form provided by Fillout, a third-party form service. If you submit an inquiry, the information you enter, and any photographs you upload, will be submitted to Fillout and made available to Grandpa's Drum for reviewing instrument offers and coordinating potential donations. This information may include contact details, location, instrument type, condition, and related descriptions, depending on the form fields presented.

Submitting an inquiry does not guarantee that an instrument will be accepted. Please do not include bank details, government identification, sensitive documents, or information unrelated to the proposed instrument donation. Grandpa's Drum's website does not currently maintain a separate database of donation submissions; the form provider is responsible for hosting the form and its submissions.

Instrument request applicant information

Our Request an Instrument page embeds a first-stage Applicant Information form provided by Fillout. If you submit this form, the information you enter is processed by Fillout and made available to Grandpa's Drum to review your interest and contact you about a possible next stage. The form may ask for applicant and contact information. Submission does not guarantee approval, equipment availability, or a gift or loan of an instrument. Please use a parent or guardian's contact details when applying for a minor and do not submit children's identifying details or sensitive financial documents.

General contact messages

Our Contact page embeds a general inquiry form provided by Fillout. If you submit a message, your contact details and message contents are processed by Fillout and made available to Grandpa's Drum to review and respond when appropriate. Please do not send identifying details about children, financial records, identity documents, or other sensitive information through this form.

Volunteer inquiries

Online volunteer inquiries are not yet open. The Get Involved page is currently informational only.

Technical information

Like most websites, the hosting infrastructure may process technical request information such as IP addresses, browser details, requested pages, and timestamps for delivery, security, and troubleshooting. Retention and access depend on the hosting and server configuration. We do not promise that technical logs are never collected.

External services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and photographs from Unsplash-hosted image URLs. The donation, instrument request, and contact pages also load embeds from Fillout. Visiting these pages may cause your browser to contact the service providers, which may receive technical request information according to their own policies. Information entered into these embedded forms is processed by Fillout. We do not currently advertise a separate third-party analytics or marketing tracking service on this site.

Retention, access, and privacy questions

Donation information is used to evaluate and coordinate instrument offers; applicant information is used to assess interest in requesting an instrument and arrange appropriate follow-up; and contact messages are used to review and respond to general inquiries. You can use our Contact page for questions about information you have submitted. A formal submission-retention schedule is still being established, and the website does not currently offer a self-service access or deletion portal. We will update this notice as submission management procedures are finalized.

Organizational status

Grandpa's Drum is currently an internal philanthropic initiative of Fast Hands Drum Studio, not a separately incorporated nonprofit or recognized 501(c)(3) organization. Donations should not be assumed to be tax deductible. Instrument offers and requests are reviewed individually and are not guaranteed to be accepted or fulfilled.
